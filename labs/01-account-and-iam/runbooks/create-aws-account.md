# Create an AWS account

## Outcome

You have an AWS account and can sign in as the **root user** (the email and password you used at sign-up).

## Prerequisites

- An email address that is **not** already tied to another AWS root user
- A phone number AWS can use for verification
- A payment method (required for account creation; you can stay on Free Tier for these labs)
- Time: ~15 minutes

## Steps

1. Open [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/) and choose **Create an AWS Account** (or **Sign up**).
2. Enter your email, account name, and a strong password. Store them in a password manager. This identity is the **root user**.
3. Complete contact and payment verification when AWS asks.
4. Choose **Basic support** (free) unless you have a reason to pick something else.
5. Wait for the confirmation email, then sign in to the AWS Management Console as root.

   - **Verify:** The console loads and the account menu shows your email (root).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email already in use | That email is already a root user on another account | Use a different email or recover the existing account |
| Card declined | Bank block or wrong details | Try another card; contact your bank |
| Console says account not fully activated | Verification still pending | Check email/SMS; wait and retry |

## Teardown

Keep the account. Do not delete it—you need it for the rest of the lab.
